## Post-mortem 2026-10-16 AMZN
- **Directional hit, magnitude miss.** Every analyst called *down*; actual close 250.0 was down 1.87% vs prior, but the blend only committed to -0.74% (252.88). The desk was right on sign, timid on size.
- **Herding killed us.** Four of five analysts clustered at 252.47–252.50 — a tight consensus anchored to the -0.64% premarket gap and SMA20 (~253.41) as a 'support floor.' News was the lone outlier *up* at 253.80, dragging the mean higher and worsening the miss.
- **Over-weighted signal:** the premarket gap and SMA20 support zone — treated as a landing target when it was actually a level price sliced through. Under-weighted: realized vol (1.49–1.7% EWMA implied a ~1-2% move was fully in play, yet nobody sized to the downside tail).
- **Root cause:** consensus mean-reversion bias — all analysts anchored to the same two levels and refused to extrapolate the clean series of lower highs (263.70→255.60) into a real breakdown.
